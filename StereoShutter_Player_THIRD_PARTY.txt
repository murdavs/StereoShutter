StereoShutter Player - software of others
=========================================

edge264
-------

video decoder by Thibault Raffaillac (https://github.com/tvlabs/edge264), built into it. Its

VLC media player
----------------
Not included. The player loads the VLC media player 3 installed on the computer (its libvlc.dll)
to decode videos and play their sound. VLC is free software by VideoLAN (https://www.videolan.org),
under the GNU GPL (the program) and the GNU LGPL (libvlc and most of its modules).
