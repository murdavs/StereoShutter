# Changes

## StereoShutter 0.18.111.209 — Samsung glasses (experimental), steadier glasses, HAGS, 3D photos, 32-bit games and more refresh rates — 2026-11-14

One download, three folders: **StereoShutter 0.18.111.209** (with StereoShutter_host.dll and StereoShutter_host32.dll 0.18.32, StereoShutter_Feedback 0.18.79 and the tools), **StereoShutter Player 1.0.4** and **StereoShutter Photos 1.0.3**, each viewer with its own copy of StereoShutter. It brings together everything since 0.18.83; the versions in between were not published (the list at the end says what each one changed). The full text is in the [release notes](RELEASE_NOTES.md). **StereoShutter Manager 0.1.116** is supplied as a separate ZIP in this release; the standalone StereoShutter ZIP also works independently. See [Manager release notes](MANAGER_RELEASE_NOTES.md).

### 📺 Samsung SSG-5100GB glasses (experimental)

- **A second kind of glasses** (`[Emitter] backend = nvidia | bluetooth`): Samsung SSG-5100GB active 3D TV glasses over Bluetooth, with a TP-Link UB500 USB adapter and TP-Link's installed driver, on an OLED monitor (`[Bluetooth] glasses_model`, `display_mode`, `monitor`, `oled_confirmed`, `refreshes_per_eye`). No NVIDIA emitter; NVIDIA 3D Vision is unchanged and stays the default.
- **Modes:** 240 Hz with two refreshes per eye and 360 Hz with three, both 60 cycles a second.
- **The adapter** is prepared for the glasses when a session starts and its driver is put back when it ends, by a helper of StereoShutter's own with administrator rights (one Windows prompt). Its firmware is read from TP-Link's installed driver and checked; it is not part of the download. The glasses join StereoShutter with their button, without Windows' Bluetooth pairing, and are remembered.
- **General glasses profile** (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\StereoShutter\glasses.ini`): the glasses, the monitor and the refreshes per eye, shared by every game; `[Glasses] profile = auto | general | game` in a game's StereoShutter.ini says whether the game follows it or keeps its own.
- **Calibration:** the bars with Ctrl+Alt and the arrow keys (phase and open time; Shift for ten times the step), saved for each monitor, resolution, refresh rate and HDR state; a game can keep its own values (`[Bluetooth.Samsung]`). In a game, Ctrl+Alt+K goes through the keys, the settings and the two bars.
- **Games with geo-11:** **StereoShutter_host.dll and StereoShutter_host32.dll 0.18.32** hand the game's picture to StereoShutter as complete pairs, each with its frame number, when StereoShutter asks for them (the Samsung glasses, or `[Input] katanga = no`); `[Host] pairs = no` turns it off. With NVIDIA glasses the picture comes through geo-11's channel as before.
- **StereoShutter_Setup** opens the assistant for NVIDIA 3D Vision glasses directly (`--nvidia-setup`). `StereoShutter.exe --settings` opens StereoShutter's own window to choose the glasses and the monitor.
- New read-only commands for other programs: `--bluetooth-status`, `--bluetooth-calibration`, `--nvidia-calibration-query`.

### 🎮 DirectX 12 games and StereoShutter Manager

- **StereoShutter Engine fixes** bring DirectX 12 games in geometric 3D, each eye rendered from its own camera, with ray tracing, DLSS Super Resolution and DLSS Frame Generation in both eyes where the game has them. With shutter glasses the pairs reach StereoShutter complete and numbered. The Witcher 3 (DirectX 12) is the first Engine title. Game fixes are separate releases and are not included in this ZIP.
- **StereoShutter Manager 0.1.116** manages compatible Engine and community geo-11 fixes and glasses setup using this StereoShutter and its general profiles. It is a separate ZIP in this release. Version 0.1.116 shows concise fix changes in seven interface languages, reviewed EN/ES Witcher 3 instructions and a clearer layout without duplicated languages. These are Manager presentation changes, not changes to game shaders. [Manager notes](MANAGER_RELEASE_NOTES.md) · [Detailed workflow](docs/STEREOSHUTTER_GUIDE.md#stereoshutter-manager).

### 🕶️ Glasses timing in games

- 🛡️ **Hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling (HAGS)**: StereoShutter runs with it (before, it refused to start: "HAGS is on"). With HAGS on, over a game, the Player or Photos, it asks Windows (UAC) for administrator rights for a helper of its own (StereoShutter.exe started again with `--gpu-realtime`), which acts only on a process of the same program file and puts it in the graphics card's realtime scheduling class while it runs; the high class StereoShutter sets for itself never lowers it. Answered no, StereoShutter runs all the same and the report and a red notice say that its images may come late. `[Output] hags_realtime = auto | no`. With the realtime class, what `queue = auto` and `engine = auto` changed to is kept for the game's next starts, as without HAGS; without the class, for that session only.
- 🧱 **Hitch guard**: after a hitch longer than the queue, from an image handed over with less time before its refresh than the screen's path needs, until Windows' presentation statistics (DXGI) show that the screen reached it, the images go without eyes: the left eye's picture in both eyes (`engine = draw` without the final image cache), black otherwise. The eye slots, the pairs and the glasses go on as planned; after 200 ms, or 4 × (queue + 4) images, the eyes come back anyway. Before, those images reached the screen one to three refreshes late, and each one was seen by the other eye. The report has a line for each hitch.
- ⏹️ **A Present held through its refresh**: when StereoShutter's Present comes back a refresh or more after the hand-over, with its eye's refresh already begun, the black or neutral image after it is presented with `DXGI_PRESENT_RESTART`, which drops that late image instead of letting the screen show it in the other eye's slot. A real eye image is never put in its place.
- 〰️ **Drift guard**: the hitch guard also starts when the screen stays behind the plan: two images planned after a delay was seen that still land later than planned, in the other eye's slot, arm it for the next eye slot. Its time limits count from the latest statistic while the screen keeps arming it. `[Diagnostics] drift_guard = no` turns it off.
- 🪟 **The window in front changes**: Windows then starts or stops composing StereoShutter's picture and the screen shifts by two refreshes for 0.25–0.3 s. The window in front is read with every image (about a microsecond); from the change, neutral images until DXGI confirms the new plan, at least 0.5 s after the change and 0.25 s after the last image seen off the plan, and never more than 2 s of waiting for quiet. Before, a burst of images in the other eye's slot at each change.
- 🔢 **DXGI's refresh count one behind its vblank times** (a refresh held two periods and counted once): when 8 samples in a row are all exactly one refresh later than the clock's line puts their counts, on a full and steady line, the clock adds one to DXGI's counts from then on and keeps its line, the plan and the glasses' timing (no pause of the eye commands, no new emitter configuration). Any other kind of run starts the clock again as before. The report counts "DXGI's counts renumbered".
- ⏩ **The rest of an eye slot goes out at once**: the black refresh and the image's other refreshes go to the swap chain right after the slot's first image; the message pump, the window and key checks, the once-a-second work (DWM's and NVIDIA's status, the GPU counters) and the overlays wait for the next slot. An image that ends up planned for another slot after a stall is no longer sent as this slot's other refresh.
- 🪶 **Present hold** (`[Output] present_hold = auto | yes | no`, auto by default): with `engine = draw` and three refreshes per eye (image, image, black), each eye image is presented once for its two refreshes (sync interval 2) and the black refresh is shown from a back buffer that already holds black (FLIP_SEQUENTIAL keeps the buffers' contents): two jobs for the graphics card a cycle instead of six, 240 presents a second instead of 360, and the same on the screen. Each Present's buffer is DXGI's count of presents; a kept black is checked on the card by reading back one row of it (every one until 16 have read black, then one every 2 s), and anything doubtful clears it as before. The hold pauses while another window is in front or the desktop composes the picture, and three pulls of the plan in 60 s start the picture again without it. `yes` also with two refreshes per eye (only the black kept).
- 🎯 **The plan follows the screen**: when the screen shows images earlier than planned twice in a row, with no gap in the chain, the plan is pulled back to the screen (`[Diagnostics] plan_pull`). Before, after some hitches the images kept landing a refresh or two early for the rest of the session: swapped eyes.
- 🐢 **Half rate is no longer for good**: `pace_game = auto` held a game at half the cycles for the rest of the run (stuck at 30 fps after a loading screen or a tutorial). Now it tries one frame per cycle again after 10 s of the game waiting 60 % of its period or more (30 s at 47 % for games capped at 60); a try that does not hold for 60 s goes back and doubles its wait (up to 320 s). `[Diagnostics] pace_retry = no` gives the old rule.
- 📉 **The automatic queue comes down** (`queue = auto`): at a raised level, the seconds of play with every image done within 80 % of the time the level below would give are counted, over runs and sessions too (`queue_auto_calm` in the game's StereoShutter.ini); after 600 of them the game's next start begins one level lower (a level is one eye interval: 5.6 ms at 90 cycles, 8.3 ms at 60). A lowered level (`queue_auto_lowered = 1`) goes back up at its first second of flicker from too little margin, and then the next lowering needs twice the seconds (`queue_auto_calm_needed`, up to 4800). A second with images off their refresh that is not too little margin is not counted as calm (and starts the count again when the measured times show that the level below would have missed).
- 📈 **Going up without restarting** (`[Output] queue_raise = auto | in_place | restart`): StereoShutter's swap chain is made with the next level's back buffers too, and a raise sets the next level's frame latency on the same swap chain at the first refresh of an eye slot: the 3D, the clock, the emitter and the game link go on. Where it cannot (its own window, no buffers for that level, Windows not taking it at once), the picture starts again as before, and the report says why. auto = in place where those buffers take at most 2 % of the graphics card's own memory.
- 🔬 **Too little margin, measured** (`[Output] queue_auto_gpu_done = exact | seen`): where the report measures when the graphics card really finished each eye image (a fence after each image, watched by a thread of its own; a report file and `engine = draw`), a second counts as too little margin only if those times say so too. Elsewhere, an image seen done only at a look that came late (the presenting thread held up) counts as no later than the look before plus an eye slot and a refresh: a stall of the whole computer is not a slow card, and no queue covers it.
- ⚙️ **The automatic engine** (`engine = auto`): the first steady second in which the graphics card held one of StereoShutter's images longer than the whole queue while the game ran on (a hitch of the game) calls for copy at once, where each eye's last finished image is shown again without work from the card. Copy is written for the game's next starts (`engine_auto_start = copy`) only after 120 s of play without flicker from too little margin; if it misses before, draw again for the rest of the session, with a red notice. While draw is in use with `engine = auto`, such flicker calls for copy before it raises the queue.
- 🎚️ **The automatic GPU priority** (`gpu_priority_absolute = auto`) now also runs where the game's picture is copied on StereoShutter's own device (`gpu_priority_scope = pipeline`: the output device takes the absolute level); "done early" allows for the time it takes to see an image done (an eye interval, plus 1 ms), so priority 16 is the rule and 17, which interrupts the game's work on the card for each image, comes only when needed.
- 📡 **The emitter**: the report says how late the emitter thread's timer woke at most, and why an eye command was skipped (the thread woke late, or found the moment already past).
- 🪫 **Less work with the report off** (the standard): the kernel's vblank listener and the scan line reader, which serve only the report, start only when the report goes to a file; so do the GPU completion watch and the once-a-second read of the game's colour space through the Katanga channel.

### 🌈 HDR

- ☀️ **Bright-area limit** (`[Output] hdr_bfi_limit = off | static | scene`, off by default; only with HDR output and black refreshes between the images; on the game's picture, HDR or SDR, never on StereoShutter's texts and panels or the 3D photo). The load of a pixel is the mean of its linear red, green and blue: below the knee (`hdr_bfi_limit_knee` × `hdr_bfi_limit_nits`; 0.6 × the monitor's full-screen luminance by default) nothing changes; from four times the knee the pixel is scaled by a gain, the three channels alike (hue, saturation and a bright area's gradation kept); a smooth step in between. `scene`: the gain for each stereo pair, measured on the graphics card before the HDR pass (a 256 × 128 target averaged down by the card's mip generation), so that the pair's mean load is `hdr_bfi_limit_nits` (auto: the monitor's full-screen luminance); pictures already below it are untouched, both eyes get the same, nothing is read back to the processor. `static`: always `hdr_bfi_limit_gain` (0.45 to 1). Keys: **Ctrl+Alt+End** on/off, **Ctrl+Alt+Home** level 100, 85, 70, 55 % (only taken with static or scene; for the session). Off, the HDR pass is the same as before.
- 🐌 **NVIDIA's HDR state**: `[Input] nvapi_hdr_reads = needed | always`. needed (the default): the display's HDR state is read during play only while the game's picture says nothing about its colours itself (neither a game layer nor the host DLL says them), the only time it can count; a slow read of the driver held StereoShutter's images for as long as it lasted (35–55 ms seen). always: once a second, as before.
- 🎨 **An 8-bit picture is never taken for HDR10** from the display's HDR state (an 8-bit picture cannot hold PQ): with Windows HDR on, a game through geo-11's Katanga channel that does not say its colours and sends 8 bits could be shown as HDR10 a second after StereoShutter's picture took the display.

### 📸 3D photos of games

- **Ctrl+Alt+P** saves the game's picture at once in Pictures\StereoShutter, as a 3840×1080 side-by-side JPG (for 3D photo programs, VR viewers and StereoShutter Photos), a side-by-side JPEG XR in HDR (games with HDR) and a 1920×1080 red-cyan anaglyph with Dubois' colours, whichever are ticked. Each eye fits whole into its 1920×1080, with black bars for wider or narrower games; without StereoShutter's texts, and with the game's own light.
- **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P** opens "Settings for this game" at what the photo saves; the choice is saved for that game (`[Photo]` in StereoShutter.ini). The photo key also works during the quick setup of the glasses, and a photo taken just before StereoShutter's picture starts again, or just before the game closes, is no longer lost without a word. `[Photo] folder` can be written between double quotes, which a folder whose name has `;` or `#` needs.

### 🎮 32-bit games and geo-11

- **StereoShutter_host32.dll** does for 32-bit DirectX 11 games with geo-11 what StereoShutter_host.dll does for 64-bit ones (the 3D over the game's window, the start with the game, the doorman, the game's own HDR), except `[Host] fullscreen_compat` and `nvapi_hdr_route`, which stay 64-bit only. Before, a 32-bit game with StereoShutter in its geo-11 did not start.
- **StereoShutter_geo-11_Setup** tells from geo-11's d3d11.dll whether the game is 32 or 64-bit and connects the DLL for it; if geo-11 and the game's programs are not of the same kind, it says so and changes nothing.
- **Fixes made for 3DMigoto Loader**: the setup looks at the folder's programs and at `[Loader] target` in d3dx.ini. A loader folder is set up as before, and the setup says how it is played: StereoShutter_Play first, then the game with the loader (StereoShutter's picture comes in a window of its own over the game, which then needs an FPS limit). A warning when the loader's target is not in the folder; in a folder with neither a program of the game nor the loader, it says where the files go and changes nothing.

### 🖥️ Refresh rates

- **300 Hz at 75 cycles a second**: below 360 Hz, two refreshes per eye (before, three from 297 Hz: 50 cycles).
- **371 to 555 Hz**: three refreshes per eye, the image twice and then black (63 cycles a second at 380 Hz, 80 at 480 Hz, 90 at 540 Hz; glasses at 124 to 185 Hz). Not yet tried on such a monitor.
- **3D Vision monitors at a refresh rate their NVIDIA table does not have**, from 198 Hz (a 240 Hz monitor at 240 Hz): the black refresh of other monitors, set up with the assistant, without LightBoost. Not yet tried. LightBoost stays at the table's modes.

## StereoShutter 0.18.83 — first public release — 2026-10-11

One download, three folders: **StereoShutter 0.18.83** (with StereoShutter_host.dll 0.18.29, StereoShutter_Feedback 0.18.77 and the tools), **StereoShutter Player 1.0.2** and **StereoShutter Photos 1.0.2**. The full text is in the [release notes](RELEASE_NOTES.md).

- 📷 **StereoShutter Photos 1.0.2**: 3D photos (MPO, JPS, PNS, side-by-side and top/bottom pictures) through the glasses, a whole folder or a slideshow, full screen or in a window.
- 🎬 **StereoShutter Player 1.0.2**: Blu-ray 3D playback from the film's start, with timeline, seeking and external subtitles synchronized to the 3D pictures; error handling for damaged media and subtitle pictures.
- 📖 **Illustrated manuals** in English and Spanish for the three programs.
- 🩺 **StereoShutter_Feedback 0.18.77**: Windows 10-compatible report dialogs and one-session capture in a ZIP of at most 5 MB. The user reviews and sends the report by e-mail; there is no automatic sending.
- 🎯 **Supplied settings with the automatic modes on**: output margin (`queue = auto`), GPU priority (`gpu_priority_absolute = auto`) and, for a game that fills the graphics card, a part of it kept for StereoShutter's images (`engine = auto`); 60 cycles a second at 360 Hz, the steadier (90 with `refreshes_per_eye = 2`). Reports off.
- 🎮 **Over a game**: StereoShutter waits for the game's picture and for the window the game really draws in, and follows the game when it makes a new window.
- 🧩 **geo-11 setup**: exclusive full screen and the 3D Vision profile of fixes made for 3D Vision turned off, geo-11's library loading kept for the games that need it, and a fault of two known geo-11 versions fixed, all with undo.
- ⌨️ **Keys**: a key without Alt (Ctrl+T) is taken only while the game or StereoShutter is in front.
- 🎮 **StereoShutter_Play**: the game launcher with a game-controller icon.

For games, install the compatible game-specific 3D fix from [HelixMod](https://helixmod.blogspot.com/search/label/geo-11) first, then connect StereoShutter. The game fixes are separate downloads, not included in this package.
